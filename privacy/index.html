Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Better Built Apps LLC ("Better Built Apps," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website, betterbuiltapps.com (the "Site").

Who we are

  • Name: Better Built Apps LLC
  • Address: 7901 4th St N STE 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702
  • Contact: the contact form on this Site

Our apps have their own policies

This policy covers the Site only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy describing exactly what that app does with data. For Lifting Lab, see the Lifting Lab Privacy Policy.

What we collect

The Site has no accounts, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

The only information we receive is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, your email address, and your message. We use it solely to read and reply to your message. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to any mailing list.

Service providers

  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form submissions to us by email. Its handling of that data is described in the Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a reasonable record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time through the contact form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Use the contact form to make a request, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version here with a new effective date.
